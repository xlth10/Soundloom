# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our commitment / 我们的承诺

We are committed to a respectful, accessible, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, disability, appearance, or personal characteristics.

我们致力于建立尊重、无障碍、无骚扰的社区，不因背景、身份、经验、残障、外貌或个人特征区别对待任何人。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy; never post another person’s recording, transcript, credentials, or identifying data.
- Accept product boundaries and maintainer decisions even when a proposal is not adopted.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and AI assistance relevant to a contribution.
- Help make discussions understandable to both Chinese- and English-speaking contributors when practical.

- 保持友善、具体和建设性。
- 讨论观点和代码，不攻击个人。
- 尊重隐私，不发布他人的录音、转写、凭据或身份信息。
- 即使方案未被采纳，也应尊重产品边界和维护者决定。
- 披露与贡献相关的利益冲突和 AI 辅助情况。
- 在可行时帮助中英文贡献者理解讨论。

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受行为

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private data, impersonation, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

项目不容忍骚扰、歧视、威胁、性化关注、人肉搜索、故意披露隐私、冒充他人或持续扰乱社区。

## Enforcement / 执行

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to [krystalwy10@gmail.com](mailto:krystalwy10@gmail.com); do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue.

维护者可在必要时编辑或删除内容、拒绝贡献、暂时限制参与或永久封禁。行为问题请私下发送至 [krystalwy10@gmail.com](mailto:krystalwy10@gmail.com)，不要在 Issue 中公开敏感证据。

This document is intentionally concise for the project’s preview stage and should be reviewed before opening a public community.

本文为项目预览阶段的精简版本，建立公开社区前应再次审核。
