# Soundloom Name and Brand Policy / Soundloom 名称与品牌政策

The Apache-2.0 license applies to covered source code; it does not grant trademark rights or permission to present a modified build as the official Soundloom app.

Apache-2.0 许可证适用于其覆盖的源代码，但不授予商标权，也不允许把修改后的构建描述为官方 Soundloom App。

Unless the owner explicitly states otherwise in writing, the following are reserved brand assets:

- the name **Soundloom** and confusingly similar product names;
- official app icons and logo treatments distributed by the maintainer;
- product screenshots under `assets/screenshots/`, official App Store screenshots, preview videos, badges, product descriptions, and launch artwork;
- official account names, domains, signing identities, and App Store listings.

除非权利人另有书面声明，以下内容属于保留品牌资产：Soundloom 名称及易混淆名称、正式 App 图标与 Logo、App Store 截图和预览、商店文案、启动视觉、官方账号、域名、签名身份与商店页面。

## Permitted use / 允许使用

You may truthfully state that software is “based on Soundloom” or “a fork of Soundloom,” link to the original project, and reproduce the project name as necessary for attribution or discussion.

你可以如实说明软件“基于 Soundloom”或“是 Soundloom 的 fork”，链接到原项目，并为署名或讨论合理使用项目名称。

## Redistributed builds / 重新分发的构建

A redistributed or modified app should use a different product name, bundle identifier, app icon, developer identity, privacy policy, and support channel. It must not imply endorsement or App Store continuity with the official build.

重新分发或修改后的 App 应使用不同的产品名称、Bundle ID、图标、开发者身份、隐私政策和支持渠道，不得暗示得到官方认可，也不得冒充官方 App Store 版本的延续。

## Current status / 当前状态

No trademark registration is asserted by this document. Before public release, the legal owner should be identified and this policy should be reviewed for the intended jurisdictions.

本文不声明 Soundloom 已完成商标注册。公开发布前，应明确合法权利人，并根据目标发行地区审核本政策。
